# NVIDIA Blackwell and Wave of Data Center Scale Platforms Power the Trillion-Parameter Al Era

NVIDIA Blackwell enables real-time generative Al on trillionparameter large language models at up to 25x lower TCO and energy consumption than its predecessor

Blackwelldelivers up to 4x faster training and 3Ox faster inference than the H100

The unit of compute for the biggest LLMs is the GB200 NVL72- a multi-node, liquid-cooled, rack-scale system combining 36 GB200 Superchips to act as a single GPU

The NVIDIA B200 GPU is the world's most powerful chip

Amazon,Google,Meta, Microsoft, OpenAl, Oracle, Tesla and xAl are among the many organizations expected to adopt Blackwell
